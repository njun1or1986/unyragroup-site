# Short-Term Sales Plan For 100+ Countries

Prepared for Unyra Group LLC on 2026-04-15.

## Objective

Build a real garlic export sales engine capable of generating qualified conversations in 100+ countries in the short term and scaling toward 190+ countries over time.

## Commercial Model

Unyra should not sell one generic item to every market. The fastest route to conversion is to run three parallel offers:

- `Offer A`: fresh whole garlic for importers, wholesalers, market distributors, and supermarket sourcing teams.
- `Offer B`: peeled refrigerated garlic for foodservice, fresh-cut operators, retail convenience packs, and central kitchens.
- `Offer C`: frozen/IQF garlic for processors, industrial kitchens, QSR suppliers, and ingredient manufacturers.

## What Must Exist Before Outreach

Unyra should standardize a sales kit with:

- company presentation;
- garlic spec sheets by caliber and format;
- packaging options;
- MOQ by product;
- Incoterms and lead times;
- phytosanitary and compliance documents;
- residue and microbiology framework;
- cold-chain handling sheet for peeled refrigerated garlic;
- industrial application sheet for frozen/IQF garlic.

## Outreach Motion

### Fresh garlic

- who to contact: procurement, imports, category managers, wholesale buyers.
- first message: focus on price stability, caliber consistency, supply continuity, and origin flexibility.

### Peeled refrigerated garlic

- who to contact: foodservice, central kitchens, fresh-cut produce buyers, convenience category teams.
- first message: focus on labor savings, cold-chain control, shelf life, and ready-to-use packs.

### Frozen/IQF garlic

- who to contact: processors, industrial kitchens, QSR suppliers, sauce and frozen food manufacturers.
- first message: focus on yield, consistency, shelf life, and formulation convenience.

## KPIs

Track weekly:

- new verified leads added;
- countries with at least 20 named targets;
- contacts with direct email;
- contacts with phone plus website;
- first outreach sent;
- replies received;
- quotes issued;
- samples requested;
- active negotiations;
- countries with first sale closed.

## Fastest Path To Real Sales

The short-term goal should be:

1. close distributor/importer relationships in 20 anchor markets;
2. use those wins to build credibility in adjacent countries;
3. extend into 100+ countries using a cluster model rather than one-by-one random outreach.
