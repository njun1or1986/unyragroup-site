# Paid Customs Intelligence Platforms For Garlic Lead Generation

Prepared for Unyra Group LLC on 2026-04-16.

## Short answer

If Unyra will buy only one paid customs platform for global garlic business development, the best choice is `Descartes Datamyne`.

Why:

- it is the strongest option I found for truly global coverage and commodity-level analysis;
- it is built for finding buyers and sellers by HS code and country;
- its official product pages explicitly position it for sales lead generation and global trade analytics;
- its coverage is broader than the more U.S.-centric tools.

## Best single paid platform

## 1. Descartes Datamyne

Best for:

- global garlic buyer discovery by `HS 070320` and adjacent processed garlic codes;
- country-by-country market mapping;
- identifying importers, exporters, suppliers, and shipment patterns;
- serious long-term commercial intelligence.

What the official site says:

- Descartes Datamyne says its global trade analytics product contains datasets for `more than 190+ countries`, with current-year data refreshed weekly.
- The platform says it helps identify global buyers and sellers by commodity and country.
- The main Datamyne site also states users can discover new buyers including `contact names and emails`.

Why it is the best single buy for Unyra:

- Unyra wants maximum planetary coverage, not just U.S. bills of lading.
- Garlic requires looking across many origins and many destination markets at once.
- Datamyne is the strongest fit for building a world-scale garlic intelligence map from one platform.

Main limitation:

- pricing is generally sales-led rather than transparent self-serve.

## Best sales-prospecting alternative

## 2. Trademo Intel

Best for:

- fast buyer discovery;
- verified buyer/supplier profiles;
- sales prospecting with contact enrichment;
- operational use by a commercial team.

What the official site says:

- Trademo advertises `3B+ shipments`, `15M+ buyer & supplier profiles`, and `1.5M+ new shipments added daily`.
- Its pricing page explicitly includes `verified contact information of buyers and suppliers`.
- It also publishes entry pricing, including an `Essential` tier at `$1,500/dataset` billed annually and a `Regional` tier at `$4,999` billed annually.

Why it matters for Unyra:

- for garlic lead generation specifically, Trademo is one of the best platforms for turning shipment intelligence into outreach lists quickly.
- it is especially useful when the goal is to build buyer lists with enough commercial detail for direct prospecting.

Main limitation:

- for deepest cross-country customs coverage and commodity analytics, Datamyne is still the stronger single-platform choice.

## Best value secondary platform

## 3. Volza

Best for:

- broad global scanning;
- importer/exporter discovery by product keyword and HSN/HS code;
- cost-effective exploration of many countries.

What the official site says:

- Volza says it compiles verified trade data from `203 countries`.
- Its company profile pages emphasize verified buyers and supplier discovery.

Why it matters for Unyra:

- Volza is very useful for garlic because it is fast for exploring buyer lists in many countries and surfacing active trading companies.
- it is often easier to use than heavier enterprise systems when the immediate goal is prospecting.

Main limitation:

- the contact and coverage experience can vary by country and dataset depth.

## Strong enterprise alternative

## 4. Panjiva Supply Chain Intelligence

Best for:

- enterprise-grade supply chain intelligence;
- competitor/supply chain mapping;
- larger teams that need a polished investigative workflow.

What the official site says:

- Panjiva positions itself as a supply chain intelligence platform with strong global coverage.
- S&P Global also states Panjiva provides contact info for over `1 million` retailers, wholesalers, and e-commerce businesses, including senior-level contacts.

Why it matters for Unyra:

- Panjiva is excellent when you need relationship mapping and stronger enterprise workflows.

Main limitation:

- it is not the best single choice if the primary goal is maximum possible world coverage for garlic leads at scale.

## Not the best one-platform choice for Unyra

## 5. ImportGenius

Best for:

- U.S.-heavy shipment analysis;
- teams focused on a narrower set of customs datasets.

What the official site says:

- ImportGenius pricing states shipment data across `25+ countries`.

Why it ranks lower here:

- Unyra wants the broadest possible worldwide garlic-buyer reach.
- `25+ countries` is materially weaker than the planetary ambition of the project.

## My recommendation by budget

### If buying one platform only

Buy `Descartes Datamyne`.

### If buying two platforms

Buy:

- `Descartes Datamyne` for global market mapping and HS-driven trade intelligence;
- `Trademo Intel` for faster commercial lead generation and contact enrichment.

### If buying three platforms

Buy:

- `Descartes Datamyne`
- `Trademo Intel`
- `Volza`

This stack is the strongest practical combination I found for:

- global garlic market intelligence;
- importer discovery by country;
- shipment history;
- contact enrichment;
- faster sales execution.

## Suggested buying decision for Unyra

Because Unyra has access to multiple origins including China, Egypt, Spain, Argentina, Brazil, and Turkey, the best move is:

1. use `Descartes Datamyne` as the global intelligence backbone;
2. use `Trademo Intel` as the commercial prospecting layer;
3. keep the public-web enrichment workflow running in parallel to lower customer acquisition cost.
