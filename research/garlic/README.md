# Garlic Research Pack

Files in this folder:

- `garlic-market-intelligence-2026.md`
  Strategic market report for fresh garlic, peeled refrigerated garlic, and frozen/IQF garlic.
- `fresh-garlic-global-import-ranking-2024.csv`
  Official WITS / World Bank ranking of fresh/chilled garlic import markets (`HS 070320`) for 2024.
- `priority-buyers-garlic-2026.md`
  Public-web buyer/importer appendix for priority markets and product keywords.
- `garlic-leads-database.csv`
  Verified public contacts database with company, country, phone, email, website, source, and notes.
- `garlic-country-expansion-pipeline.csv`
  174-country operational pipeline with target importer count per market.
- `garlic-short-term-sales-plan-100-countries.md`
  Practical commercial attack plan for reaching 100+ countries in the short term.
- `customs-intelligence-platforms-2026.md`
  Recommendation and comparison of paid customs intelligence platforms for world-scale garlic buyer discovery.

Recommended reading order:

1. `garlic-market-intelligence-2026.md`
2. `fresh-garlic-global-import-ranking-2024.csv`
3. `garlic-leads-database.csv`
4. `garlic-country-expansion-pipeline.csv`
5. `garlic-short-term-sales-plan-100-countries.md`
6. `customs-intelligence-platforms-2026.md`
7. `priority-buyers-garlic-2026.md`

Method note:

- Official country ranking is strong and auditable.
- Company-level buyer intelligence is strong in priority markets but should be validated before quotation and onboarding.
