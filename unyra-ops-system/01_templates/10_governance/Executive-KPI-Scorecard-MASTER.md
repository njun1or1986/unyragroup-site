## Score Calibration Matrix

| Rating Band | Score Range | Meaning | Founder Posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled | 90-100 | Business quality is strong and governable. | Protect discipline and scale carefully. |
| Advancing | 80-89 | Business quality is healthy but still needs active founder pressure. | Tighten weak areas before they grow. |
| Fragile | 70-79 | Activity is moving faster than quality in at least one critical area. | Intervene, narrow exceptions, and open controls. |
| Critical | Below 70 | Control quality is insufficient for safe scaling. | Pause expansion, escalate, and correct before release. |

## Metric Normalization Rules

- Use consistent periods, denominators, and definitions across all score categories.
- Prefer quality ratios, controlled scores, and exception rates over raw activity counts when possible.
- Mark any materially incomplete metric as provisional in the founder note.
- Anchor any judgment-based score to the linked dashboard or source record.
- Do not claim improvement if the underlying source layer is stale or low-confidence.
