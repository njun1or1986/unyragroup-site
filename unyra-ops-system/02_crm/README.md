# CRM

This layer is the founder commercial command center for UNYRA. It governs how opportunities, customers, suppliers, deals, retained outcomes, and follow-through tasks move through the operating system before revenue confidence, release exposure, supplier dependence, or founder attention widens.

## CRM Control Posture

| Control Field | Current Standard | Founder Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layer status | Active commercial control map | Use this hub when a commercial question needs the right owner room before confidence or movement widens. |
| Primary purpose | Connect pursuit, account quality, supplier confidence, deal posture, retained memory, and follow-through ownership. | Gives the founder one route map for commercial decisions without flattening live details into a single note. |
| Source rule | Update the owned lead, customer, supplier, deal, or task room first, then refresh dashboards. | Prevents pipeline optimism from outrunning source evidence. |
| Decision rule | Do not move a path to a stronger commercial posture until pricing, trust, cash, supplier, release, and owner proof support the same answer. | Keeps commercial momentum disciplined instead of vibes-led. |
| Protection rule | Keep CRM records out of protected templates and preserve approved brand/template assets. | Protects master materials while live commercial records mature. |

## Founder Control Gates

| Gate | Pass Standard | If Not Passed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing quality | Pricing governance supports quote, concession, margin, expiry, and recovery logic. | Keep offer confidence from widening. |
| Trust quality | Customer or supplier trust posture supports the release, credit, sourcing, or dependence being requested. | Keep movement controlled and refresh trust posture. |
| Cash quality | Collections, cash outlook, funding, credit, and release records carry one answer. | Block release acceleration, supplier reservation, or payment movement. |
| Supplier quality | Supplier approval, performance, capacity, backup coverage, and compliance proof support the planned demand. | Hold volume, sourcing dependence, or customer commitment. |
| Forecast quality | Revenue band matches the real proof state instead of aspiration, weighted hope, or stretch logic. | Keep the path in lead, weighted, stretch, or active watch posture. |
| Owner quality | The next action has a named owner, deadline, evidence route, and escalation standard. | Route to the founder execution queue before approving movement. |

## Protection and Escalation Rules

| Trigger | Required Action | Founder Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A lead, weighted path, or stretch path starts behaving like a live deal | Keep it outside active posture until source evidence supports promotion. | Prevents forecast optimism from becoming hidden commitment. |
| A customer request widens release, credit, pricing, or growth exposure | Route through customer room, credit release, trust posture, pricing governance, and cash controls. | Keeps relationship momentum from outrunning proof. |
| Supplier dependence widens while approval, performance, backup, or compliance proof remains incomplete | Hold supplier posture at controlled and refresh supplier approval plus supply resilience. | Prevents sourcing confidence from outrunning resilience. |
| A won deal is reused as precedent before execution proof and profitability are retained | Refresh won room, trade review, release proof, and archive path before reuse. | Keeps reference wins proof-backed. |
| Founder asks for movement but owner, deadline, or evidence route is unclear | Route to founder execution queue and owner accountability before approving. | Prevents work from moving on memory. |
| CRM notes start carrying protected template, brand, or final-doc content | Move live work to the owned room and preserve protected masters. | Maintains template and brand protections. |
