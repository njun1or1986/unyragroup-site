## Message Architecture

| Message Part | Required Standard | Approved Use | Blocked Drift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Subject line | Specific, calm, and traceable to the commercial packet or response action. | Reference Atlantic Foods, garlic offer, proforma review, response due, or packet follow-up. | Do not use urgency language that implies crisis, allocation pressure, or shipment movement. |
| Opening | Professional acknowledgment of the review item and current packet context. | Confirm the packet, offer, specification, or response window without overstating commitment. | Do not imply relationship familiarity is a substitute for proof. |
| Commercial statement | Precise summary of what is being reviewed and why the response matters. | State pricing validity, product specification, response request, and payment-structure dependency. | Do not treat high-confidence opportunity as booked revenue or approved release. |
| Action request | One clear buyer action with a due point and owner expectation. | Request receipt confirmation, commercial review, approval or rejection, and payment-structure confirmation where applicable. | Do not ask for vague comfort, informal agreement, or partial approval that could be mistaken for commitment. |
| Deadline language | Firm, neutral, and tied to validity, pricing, or release-control consequences. | Explain that stale response may require pricing or release revalidation. | Do not threaten, pressure, or suggest automatic release after silence. |
| Escalation language | Controlled and evidence-based. | Escalate through response SLA, communication control, pricing governance, and founder review when timing weakens. | Do not improvise new concessions, revised terms, or movement promises inside reminder language. |
