| --- | --- | --- |
| Permanent pricing concession or future pricing precedent. | Guarded account-entry flexibility is not a new price rule. | Founder decision, pricing review, exception expiry, and profitability recovery align. |
| Widened supplier settlement or payment movement. | Release deviation does not replace funding proof. | Funding, credit, executable-check, scenario, and risk records carry one answer. |
| Broker handoff widening or shipment acceleration. | Trust override is not movement authority. | Pre-shipment release, document control, and executable-check proof align. |
| Supplier final allocation implication. | Customer interest is not supplier allocation clearance. | Customer response, supplier packet, funding, and release gate are current. |
| CAPA or claim closure language. | Remedy and prevention evidence remain live controls. | Recovery, CAPA, traceability, supplier, and profitability records support closure. |
