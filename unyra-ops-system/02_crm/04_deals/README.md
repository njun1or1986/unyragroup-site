## Lane Promotion Rules

| Movement | Required Standard | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead to active deal | Qualified counterparty, buyer need, pricing boundary, evidence gap, owner, and escalation timebox are current. | `02_crm/04_deals/active/` |
| Weighted forecast to active deal | Buyer acceptance, supplier-slot proof, cash and release guardrails, and source record ownership justify a live room. | `02_crm/04_deals/active/` |
| Active deal to won | Buyer commitment, pricing, payment, release, execution proof, and closeout path are route-backed. | `02_crm/04_deals/won/` |
| Active deal to lost | Loss reason, evidence trail, owner learning, pricing/payment lesson, and re-entry boundary are route-backed. | `02_crm/04_deals/lost/` |
| Won or lost to re-entry | Retained memory produces a new qualified lead with corrected assumptions. | `02_crm/01_leads/` |

## Evidence Quality Gates

| Gate | Required Answer | If Weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing quality | Pricing governance supports quote, concession, margin, expiry, and recovery logic. | Keep confidence from widening. |
| Response quality | Response SLA shows dated acknowledgment, review status, decision timing, and payment structure. | Do not treat silence as acceptance. |
| Cash and release quality | Cash forecast, credit control, funding release, and pre-shipment release carry one answer. | Block capital, supplier, broker, shipment, or payment movement. |
| Forecast quality | Revenue forecast band matches actual proof state. | Keep the path as high-confidence, weighted, stretch, or lead instead of overpromoting it. |
| Retained memory quality | Won or lost records prove why the outcome happened and what should be reused or avoided. | Do not use retained memory as precedent or re-entry basis yet. |
