# Founder Operating Cadence SOP

| SOP control | Current standard |
| --- | --- |
| Status | ACTIVE SOP |
| Purpose | Define how the founder uses daily dashboards, executive decision records, weekly review, monthly review, quarterly planning, and source records as one operating rhythm. |
| Protected boundary | Cadence layers summarize and govern; they do not replace source records. |
| Founder standard | No review layer is decision-ready until the linked records are current enough to trust. |

## Operating Architecture

| Cadence layer | Operating role | Founder decision posture | Primary surface |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Daily control | Keeps the business current across sales, operations, compliance, collections, cash, and founder decisions. | Same-day awareness and routing. | `06_dashboard/01_executive/Daily-Founder-Dashboard.md` |
| Weekly executive review | Turns daily movement into judgment, priorities, and direction for the next cycle. | Movement, stall points, risk, and follow-through. | `06_dashboard/14_weekly-executive-review/Weekly-Executive-Operating-Review.md` |
| Monthly strategic review | Turns weekly judgment into broader business posture, strategic trade-offs, and direction for the next month. | Business quality, execution quality, trust behavior, cash pressure, and system discipline. | `06_dashboard/15_monthly-strategic-review/Monthly-Strategic-Business-Review.md` |
| Quarterly planning | Turns the strategic reading of the business into a 90-day commitment layer with targets, owners, and deliberate trade-offs. | Commitment, not diagnosis. | `06_dashboard/22_quarterly-operating-plan/Quarterly-Operating-Plan.md` |
| Source-record boundary | Keeps each dashboard honest by tying conclusions back to live records and evidence. | Material or disputed conclusions must be sourced before they shape action. | CRM, shipment, compliance, finance, communication, and archive records. |

## Quarterly Commitment Gate

| Quarterly review condition | Required standard |
| --- | --- |
| Planning surface | Use `06_dashboard/22_quarterly-operating-plan/Quarterly-Operating-Plan.md` once per quarter. |
| Readiness threshold | Pull the quarterly plan only after the monthly strategic review, strategic counterparty trust review, initiative tracker, revenue forecast, capital allocation review, scenario response review, assumption review, plan variance review, KPI scorecard, executive risk register, cash forecast, concentration review, and supply resilience review are current enough to trust. |
| Commitment lens | Confirm what must be true by quarter end, what quality thresholds must improve, what response and counterparty-trust thresholds must improve on strategic relationships, what live-gate or executable-check behavior must improve, what trade-offs the founder is willing to make, and who owns each cross-functional priority. |
| Initiative bridge | Use `06_dashboard/16_strategic-initiatives/Strategic-Initiative-Tracker.md` for priorities that span multiple weeks or months. |
| Initiative activation | Open or update the tracker after monthly review clarifies the top cross-functional priorities for the next cycle. |
| Weekly initiative check | Use the weekly executive review to confirm whether those initiatives are moving, stalling, or requiring founder intervention. |

## Founder Output Standards

| Review layer | Required ending output |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly executive review | A clear business posture statement; the top three founder priorities for next week; founder decisions that still need follow-through; the executable-check or closure-evidence gap that could still invalidate founder intent; and records that must be updated before the next cycle. |
| Monthly strategic review | A clear business posture statement; the top three founder priorities for next month; strategic decisions that require follow-through; the trust or response pattern that could quietly weaken execution; the operating-system or executable-check behavior gap that could quietly weaken execution; and the main watchpoint for the next monthly cycle. |
| Quarterly operating plan | A clear 90-day posture statement; top quarter priorities with named owners; strategic trust or response commitments that should reduce friction on live business; live-gate discipline commitments that should reduce founder rescue by quarter end; accepted trade-offs; and milestones that prove whether the quarter is actually working. |

## Escalation And Source-Record Rules

| Trigger | Required action | Required reflection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly review reveals unresolved cross-functional conflict | Do not bury it in notes; open or update `06_dashboard/13_founder-decisions/Executive-Decision-Board.md`, `06_dashboard/10_matter-control/Active-Matter-Control-Board.md`, or the relevant source record. | Reflect the issue in the next daily dashboard cycle. |
| Founder override is active under fragile or controlled trust | Confirm override class, what still stays blocked, recheck date, and proof required to return to standard rules through `06_dashboard/32_exception-governance/Executive-Exceptions-and-Override-Review.md`. | Carry the same override meaning through decision, trust, matter, release, and archive layers. |
| Trust-linked founder override is moving toward release | Use `06_dashboard/33_control-routing/Founder-Control-Routing-Matrix.md` to force synchronized override meaning first, `06_dashboard/34_control-integrity/Executive-Control-Integrity-and-Decision-Readiness-Review.md` second, and live functional gate third. | Movement should not start until the linked records agree. |
| Live executable check is active | Use `06_dashboard/40_executable-checks/Trust-Override-Executable-Check-Control-Board.md` to create one founder-ready operating answer across tasks, gates, evidence, and archive readiness. | Closure proof must be recoverable before founder intent is treated as satisfied. |
