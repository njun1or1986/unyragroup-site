# Compliance

This layer is the founder compliance control center for UNYRA. It connects regulatory proof, traceability, inspections, complaint recovery, CAPA, supplier approval, release evidence, and audit readiness so compliance confidence cannot widen faster than the underlying source records support.

## Compliance Control Posture

| Control Field | Current Standard | Founder Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layer status | Active proof, recovery, and supplier-control layer | Use this hub when a decision depends on compliance evidence rather than commercial narrative alone. |
| Primary purpose | Keep complaint recovery, supplier approval, traceability, inspection readiness, and release proof in one governed map. | Lets the founder find the right proof room before execution, communication, recovery, or release widens. |
| Source rule | Update the owned room first, then refresh dashboards and founder views. | Prevents a polished dashboard from outrunning the underlying proof record. |
| Decision rule | Do not call a compliance matter stable until proof chain, owner discipline, supplier posture, release packet, CAPA status, and recovery consequence support the same answer. | Keeps closure language proof-led rather than calendar-led. |
| Protection rule | Keep working evidence outside protected templates and preserve approved brand/template assets. | Protects template masters while compliance records mature. |

## Founder Release Gates

| Gate | Pass Standard | If Not Passed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traceability confidence | Retrieval evidence, lot path, shipment proof, and evidence-gap language support the same answer. | Keep challenge defense, recovery, and regulator-facing language bounded. |
| CAPA closure | Root cause, corrective action, preventive verification, owner, and next comparable-cycle proof are current. | Keep complaint or inspection posture open under CAPA governance. |
| Supplier confidence | Approval, performance, traceability, CAPA, backup sourcing, and trust posture agree. | Keep supplier approved with controls and block trust improvement. |
| Release packet confidence | Document control, pre-shipment release, funding, shipment review, and executable-check evidence align. | Do not widen movement or filing confidence. |
| Recovery confidence | Customer remedy, supplier recovery, traceability, CAPA, account impact, and margin consequence are synchronized. | Keep remedy provisional and refresh claims recovery. |
| Archive readiness | Compliance work has no live proof gap, owner action, CAPA, claim, filing, release, or retrieval issue remaining. | Keep the matter in active compliance control. |

## Protection and Escalation Rules

| Trigger | Required Action | Founder Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof gap remains open while closure, recovery, release, or supplier confidence widens | Freeze stronger language and refresh the owned proof room plus compliance dashboard. | Prevents implied compliance closure. |
| Complaint remedy moves before CAPA, traceability, supplier recovery, and margin consequence agree | Route through claims recovery, CAPA governance, account review, and profitability controls. | Prevents commercial calm from hiding recurrence or margin risk. |
| Supplier trust improves while traceability, CAPA, or backup sourcing remains incomplete | Hold trust at controlled and refresh supplier approval plus performance board. | Prevents sourcing confidence from outrunning proof. |
| Packet, release, funding, and executable-check records diverge | Stop movement expansion until control integrity is restored. | Keeps release authority evidence-backed. |
| Compliance evidence is placed in protected templates or loose working files | Move evidence to the owned compliance, operations, CRM, or dashboard record. | Preserves protected masters and keeps proof retrievable. |
| Archive is requested while any live compliance issue can still change | Return to active compliance control and archive only after closeout criteria pass. | Prevents unresolved work from leaving active view. |
