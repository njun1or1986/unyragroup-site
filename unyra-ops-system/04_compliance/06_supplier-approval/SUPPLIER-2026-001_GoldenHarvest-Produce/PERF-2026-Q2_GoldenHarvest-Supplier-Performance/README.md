## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Trigger | Immediate Move | Founder Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier confirmation compresses release, funding, or packet decisions. | Tighten confirmation deadline and refresh response escalation, supplier release packet, and shipment control tower. | Prevents timing pressure from becoming release permission. |
| Scorecard is cited to widen dependence while backup coverage remains weak. | Route to capacity module and supply resilience board. | Prevents strong current performance from masking fragile fallback depth. |
| CAPA, traceability, or claim recovery remains open while trust improvement is requested. | Keep trust tier controlled and refresh compliance, CAPA, and claims recovery records. | Prevents confidence from outrunning proof. |
| Shipment review finds weak supplier response or document evidence. | Feed result into scorecard and supplier performance board before next approval call. | Keeps supplier posture responsive to actual execution. |
| Supplier payment or movement language exceeds funding/release proof. | Freeze wording and route through funding release, pre-shipment release, and executable-check board. | Protects cash and movement discipline. |
