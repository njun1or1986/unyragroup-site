| Trigger | Required Action | Founder Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Backup-source coverage weakens or remains below 70% after the next review cycle | Keep Golden Harvest active only under controlled dependence and refresh mitigation queue. | Fallback weakness should not be disguised by good primary execution. |
| Golden Harvest concentration rises above the current 36% sourcing share | Move the decision into concentration review before new commitments are accepted. | Supplier success can create fragility if it concentrates too quickly. |
| Peak packing-slot confirmation slips past 2026-05-09 | Pause widening of garlic commitments until slot proof is restored. | Capacity confidence must be current before customer promises expand. |
| Sunrise Agro qualification expansion misses the 2026-05-28 target | Treat garlic fallback as partial standby only and update the risk register. | A named fallback without release-ready volume is not a resilience answer. |
| Supply-disruption recovery window remains above 10 days | Keep founder review on any high-value or time-sensitive garlic commitment. | Recovery timing still exceeds operating risk appetite. |
| Promotional spot demand appears before a fallback playbook exists | Route the decision to scenario review before accepting the order. | Unhedged spot demand can consume capacity that the core program needs. |
