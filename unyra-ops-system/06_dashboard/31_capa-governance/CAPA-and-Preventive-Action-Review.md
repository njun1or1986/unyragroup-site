## CAPA Stage Gates

| Stage gate | Required evidence | Founder status | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contained | Customer communication, hold or remedy path, owner, and immediate risk boundary are documented. | Customer and commercial pressure can be managed. | Stop if containment is used as closure language. |
| Root cause supported | Lot path, inspection evidence, shipment conditions, supplier input, and packaging review support one cause path. | Root-cause statement can be drafted. | Stop if narrative outruns evidence. |
| Corrective action closed | Owner action, due date, completion proof, and customer or recovery impact are recorded. | Current issue can move toward closeout. | Stop if correction lacks evidence or owner sign-off. |
| Preventive action verified | Next comparable shipment or supplier cycle shows revised control worked. | CAPA can move toward verified prevention. | Stop if verification is theoretical or advisory only. |
| Standard posture restored | Supplier, release, customer, and profitability records all reflect the verified control improvement. | System can return to normal posture. | Stop if one linked board still shows unresolved risk. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Closure language stop | Team uses closure language before root cause, correction, and prevention proof are complete. | Route to control integrity and matter control. | Keep CAPA open and require proof-pack completion. |
| Prevention proof stop | Preventive action is marked complete without next-cycle execution evidence. | Route to supplier performance and shipment release review. | Withhold standard supplier or release posture. |
| Remedy outruns prevention | Customer remedy or credit closes while CAPA proof remains weak. | Route to claims recovery and profitability review. | Keep recovery economics and supplier accountability open. |
| Supplier confidence stop | Supplier remains at normal posture despite unresolved recurrence risk. | Route to supplier performance, supply resilience, and risk register. | Tighten supplier release posture or require backup-source action. |
| Missed verification trigger | Verification date slips or evidence is incomplete. | Route to executive risk and plan variance. | Treat CAPA delay as executive control risk. |
