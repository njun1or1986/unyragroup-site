# Claims, Credits, and Recovery Review

This dashboard governs claim response, customer remedy, supplier recovery, credit discipline, CAPA linkage, and realized-margin protection. It keeps the founder from solving the customer relationship quickly while accidentally normalizing preventable margin leakage or weak supplier accountability.

## Closeout Gates

| Gate | Pass Standard | Founder Stop Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer remedy gate | Remedy range, customer language, account impact, and exposure math are current. | Do not finalize credit or replacement while exposure is still directional. |
| Supplier recovery gate | Traceability, photos, loading proof, CAPA, shipment review, and supplier response support the offset request. | Do not overclaim supplier recovery from incomplete proof. |
| CAPA gate | Root cause, corrective action, preventive verification, owner, and next comparable-cycle proof are current. | Do not call recovery closed while recurrence control is unproven. |
| Profitability gate | Credit, replacement cost, supplier offset, unrecovered loss, and margin effect are posted to trade profitability. | Do not let commercial peace hide realized economics. |
| Supplier consequence gate | Supplier performance and approval posture absorb the final claim outcome. | Do not let supplier trust improve while case evidence remains unresolved. |
| Archive gate | Claim, recovery, CAPA, supplier consequence, account posture, and economics are retrievable and aligned. | Do not archive while recovery or proof can still change. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Trigger | Immediate Move | Founder Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial team pushes remedy before supplier recovery logic is visible | Hold remedy range and refresh customer account, recovery module, and supplier proof. | Prevents fast relationship repair from hiding loss. |
| Supplier evidence package remains incomplete | Keep supplier recovery confidence bounded and refresh traceability, CAPA, shipment review, and supplier performance. | Prevents weak offset claim and future trust inflation. |
| Claim closes without profitability update | Reopen finance posting through trade profitability before closure. | Makes margin leakage visible. |
| CAPA verification remains directional | Freeze closure language and refresh CAPA governance. | Prevents recurrence risk from being disguised as resolved. |
| Account trust weakens after remedy discussion | Return to customer account review and trust posture before further credit or release movement. | Keeps relationship protection tied to controls. |
| Any linked layer contradicts recovery posture | Run control integrity before founder approval. | Protects decision quality across commercial, compliance, supplier, and finance layers. |
