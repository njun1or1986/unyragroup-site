## Capital Permission Bands

| Permission band | Condition | Founder posture | Required control action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capital-permitted | Cash, release, trust, source, and margin evidence are current and aligned. | Approve inside stated limits. | Record amount, owner, route, and next review date. |
| Capital-contained | Opportunity is valid but depends on near-term proof or narrow movement. | Permit only reversible, bounded action. | Require cash, funding, credit, and source evidence before widening. |
| Capital-watch | Opportunity is attractive but still weighted, trust-sensitive, or concentration-heavy. | Keep under review without full release. | Hold capital until forecast and source proof improves. |
| Capital-constrained | Movement would pressure buffer, release exposure, or supplier capacity. | Protect liquidity and committed work first. | Route to scenario response, risk, and founder decisions. |
| Capital-blocked | Movement asks for cash, release, or capacity without proof or while blocked actions remain. | Stop movement. | Document blocked action and proof trigger. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buffer breach stop | Deployable cash buffer falls below protected threshold or stress gap expands. | Route to cash forecast, capital allocation, and founder decisions. | Freeze non-critical releases. |
| Release proof stop | Release request lacks current cash, credit, trust, or funding proof. | Route to control integrity, credit release, and funding review. | Block movement until proof lands. |
| Weighted-upside stop | Weighted or stretch revenue asks for capital before commitment quality improves. | Route to revenue forecast and quarterly plan. | Keep capital deferred. |
| Concentration stop | Allocation increases account, supplier, product, or cash concentration without mitigation. | Route to concentration review and risk register. | Cap exposure or require diversification proof. |
| Trust-fragility stop | Trust posture weakens or blocked actions remain unresolved. | Route to trust posture, exception governance, and founder decisions. | Keep allocation narrow or blocked. |
