## Routing Stop Rules

| Stop rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No lead-control stop | Issue has more than one plausible board and no declared lead. | Route to this matrix and matter control. | Pause decision until the lead layer is named. |
| Stale-input stop | Supporting records are stale, conflicting, or incomplete. | Route to control integrity. | Do not approve from partial truth. |
| Executable ambiguity stop | Trust-linked movement is requested without executable or not-executable status. | Route to executable-check board. | Block movement until status is explicit. |
| Exception ambiguity stop | Override is requested without expiry, blocked action, or return-to-standard language. | Route to exception governance and founder decisions. | Tighten or reject the override. |
| Founder-rescue repeat | Same issue returns to founder because routing or owner behavior failed. | Route to owner accountability, adoption readiness, and plan variance as relevant. | Treat as system-use failure, not routine escalation. |
