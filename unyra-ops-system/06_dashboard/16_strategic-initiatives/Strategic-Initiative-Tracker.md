## Founder Decision Gates

| Gate | Trigger | Evidence required | Founder decision | Default posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Continue | Initiative shows proof-backed movement and no rising hidden exposure. | Current source board, named owner, next dated proof event, and no unresolved stop rule. | Keep initiative active and protect current resource allocation. | Continue with weekly proof. |
| Intensify | Initiative matters strategically but owner movement is slower than risk or opportunity requires. | Drift evidence from owner accountability, KPI scorecard, variance review, or live-gate board. | Increase founder pressure, narrow scope, or assign tighter deadline. | Intensify for one cycle only. |
| Reallocate | Initiative is strategically valid but another initiative has higher cash, margin, or control value. | Capital allocation review, cash forecast, risk register, and quarterly plan comparison. | Shift capital, founder attention, or operating owner capacity. | Reallocate only with recorded tradeoff. |
| Pause | Initiative lacks current proof, has stale owner behavior, or depends on weak controls. | Missing source refresh, unresolved dependency, or repeated founder-rescued drift. | Pause expansion until source record and proof event are refreshed. | Pause before new commitments. |
| Close | Initiative objective has become embedded in operating behavior or is no longer a strategic priority. | KPI evidence, owner accountability evidence, and updated source board showing sustained control. | Close initiative and move lessons into SOP, dashboard, or archive records. | Close only with learning captured. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stale-source stop | Any initiative source board is not current at the time of strategic review. | Route the gap to the source owner and mark the initiative as evidence-stale. | Pause expansion until the source record is refreshed. |
| Founder-rescue repeat | The same initiative needs founder intervention in two consecutive review cycles. | Route to owner accountability and plan variance review. | Intensify, narrow scope, or replace the operating owner. |
| Capital mismatch | Initiative asks for growth, payment, release, or concession support without current capital posture. | Route to capital allocation, cash forecast, and funding release review. | Hold the commitment until cash and release evidence are reconciled. |
| Live-gate bypass | Trust-linked payment, release, or override is proposed without executable-check evidence. | Route to executable-check control and funding release review. | Stop the action until the live-gate proof record is complete. |
| Narrative-only progress | Initiative update reports activity but no measurable control, KPI, or source-record movement. | Route to KPI scorecard and owner accountability review. | Reject the update and require a dated proof event. |
