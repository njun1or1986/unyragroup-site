## Trust Tier Definition Matrix

| Trust Tier | Response Pattern | Release Posture | Growth or Sourcing Posture | Founder Expectation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Institutional | Clean acknowledgments, low reminder burden, and predictable follow-through on critical matters. | Normal controlled release within standard UNYRA gates. | Growth and sourcing can scale without extra trust friction premium. | Review monthly and protect the standard. |
| Controlled | Mostly responsive, but timing still needs active management on key matters. | Release allowed with explicit deadline control and short revalidation windows. | Growth or sourcing can continue, but only with visible watch points. | Review every cycle until reliability is stronger. |
| Fragile | Repeated reminders, stage-2 escalation, or silence on release-critical matters. | Tighten release, require dated confirmation, and hold acceleration until behavior improves. | Slow account growth or activate backup sourcing until two cleaner cycles appear. | Founder should treat trust as a real operating constraint. |
| Restricted | Silence, broken commitments, or unresolved risk now undermine safe release confidence. | Hold, revalidate, or founder-approve only under exceptional logic. | Stop scaling, open fallback path, and protect continuity first. | Immediate founder intervention and short review cadence. |
