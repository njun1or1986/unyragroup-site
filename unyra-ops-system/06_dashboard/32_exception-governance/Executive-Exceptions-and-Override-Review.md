## Override Class Matrix

| Override class | When it is allowed | Required proof | Founder posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing concession override | Strategic account capture requires a concession but margin logic and recovery route are visible. | Pricing review, profitability view, forecast impact, and founder decision record. | Approve narrowly and reprice at the next clean cycle. |
| Release or credit override | Relationship timing needs movement but deposit, collection, and exposure guardrails still hold. | Credit control, cash forecast, funding release posture, and blocked-action list. | Permit only inside documented release boundary. |
| Trust-fragility override | Strategic upside is real but counterparty trust posture is not strong enough for normal-speed movement. | Trust posture matrix, counterparty response evidence, and return-to-standard trigger. | Keep rare, explicit, and time-bound. |
| Remedy or claims override | Customer protection requires temporary tolerance while recovery is pursued. | Claims recovery, supplier offset evidence, customer account posture, and margin impact. | Approve only with recovery economics and supplier accountability visible. |
| Preventive-control override | Supplier or process remains active while CAPA proof is not yet fully verified. | CAPA record, supplier performance evidence, verification date, and shipment-risk posture. | Hold standard posture until verified prevention closes. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expiry breach stop | Exception remains open past review date or proof trigger. | Route to founder decision board and active matter control. | Freeze expansion until expiry or renewal decision is explicit. |
| Blanket-permission stop | Team treats a narrow override as permission across payment, release, sourcing, or pricing. | Route to control routing, funding release, and trust posture. | Restate blocked actions and reverse unauthorized widening. |
| Recovery missing stop | Exception has no margin, cash, trust, remedy, or prevention recovery path. | Route to profitability, claims recovery, CAPA, or capital allocation as relevant. | Hold or tighten exception until recovery proof exists. |
| Founder-rescue repeat | Same exception class requires founder call twice in one cycle. | Route to owner accountability and plan variance. | Treat as owner or system-adoption failure. |
| Normalization trigger | Same exception class repeats across matters or cycles without standard-rule change. | Route to control integrity and monthly strategic review. | Decide whether to formalize, tighten, or prohibit the pattern. |
