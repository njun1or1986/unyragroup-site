## Follow-Through Maturity Ladder

| Maturity stage | Definition | Evidence required | Founder posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assigned | Owner and commitment are named. | Owner, source route, expected output, and date exist. | Useful baseline, not proof of execution. |
| Active | Owner is moving the work and source records show current activity. | Updated source record, next action, and no stale dependency. | Count as live but not closed. |
| Proof-backed | Owner has delivered evidence that changes the decision, gate, or recovery state. | Current board update, task record, source proof, and owner note. | Count as meaningful follow-through. |
| Gate-closed | Sensitive decision, payment, release, trust, or recovery gate is closed without founder rescue. | Executable-check, decision, release, or matter-control closeout. | Count as high-rigor accountability. |
| System-improving | Owner action reduces repeat founder intervention or converts learning into the operating system. | Adoption, SOP, dashboard, variance, or risk record reflects the learning. | Count as mature owner behavior. |

## Commitment Proof Standard

| Proof type | Minimum evidence | Weak substitute to reject | Founder read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash follow-through | Collection promise, date, owner, escalation path, and updated cash or release posture. | General confidence that payment will land. | Cash accountability is real when timing and release posture change. |
| Commercial follow-through | Qualified buyer action, forecast band movement, next date, and owner note. | Pipeline narrative without stage proof. | Diversification is proof, not activity. |
| Sourcing follow-through | Backup-source evidence, capacity confirmation, assumption status, and next proof date. | Supplier comfort without readiness proof. | Fallback quality must be tested before pressure. |
| Executable-check follow-through | Current task record, executable-check board answer, gate status, and closure proof. | Founder availability or chat recap. | Live-gate ownership must close in the system. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stale proof stop | Owner status is positive but source proof is stale or missing. | Route to control integrity and weekly review. | Reject status language until source evidence refreshes. |
| Founder-rescue repeat | Same owner path requires founder intervention twice in one cycle. | Route to owner accountability, adoption readiness, and plan variance. | Convert rescue into formal owner correction. |
| Live-gate owner stop | Trust-linked movement lacks current owner-led executable-check closure. | Route to executable-check board and funding release review. | Block movement until proof lands. |
| Cross-layer drift trigger | Same commitment appears in several boards but still does not move. | Route to matter control and founder decisions. | Name one lead owner and one lead route. |
| Missed proof date trigger | Owner misses proof date without escalation before the deadline. | Route to risk register and plan variance. | Treat miss as executive risk, not ordinary delay. |
