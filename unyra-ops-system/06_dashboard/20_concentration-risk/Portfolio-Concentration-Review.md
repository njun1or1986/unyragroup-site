## Concentration Exposure Bands

| Exposure band | Condition | Founder posture | Required control action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic focus | Concentration is intentional, profitable, cash-clean, and supported by backup paths. | Permit growth while monitoring. | Keep source records current and protect margins. |
| Controlled watch | Concentration is useful but still depends on one customer, supplier, region, or product family. | Allow only with dated mitigation proof. | Link to pipeline, supply resilience, cash, and risk controls. |
| Capital constraint | Concentration could absorb scarce cash, release capacity, credit, or founder attention. | Do not widen exposure casually. | Require capital allocation and cash forecast support before expansion. |
| Fragility warning | Concentration creates synchronized downside across cash, revenue, supply, or trust. | Treat as executive risk. | Route to risk register, scenario response, and plan variance. |
| Stop-expansion | Concentrated exposure lacks proof, owner, fallback, or recovery path. | Pause further reliance. | Hold commitments until diversification or containment evidence improves. |

## Escalation and Stop Rules

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash concentration stop | Top-account cash dependence rises or collection proof weakens while exposure remains high. | Route to cash forecast, collections control, credit release, and capital allocation. | Hold release expansion until cash proof improves. |
| Product concentration stop | Garlic margin exposure rises without backup source or adjacent SKU proof. | Route to supply resilience, trade profitability, and risk register. | Cap promise aggression until fallback proof is current. |
| Customer exception stacking | One account requires pricing, release, trust, remedy, or CAPA exceptions in the same cycle. | Route to exception governance and matter control. | Consolidate exception map before new approvals. |
| Pipeline proof failure | Diversification remains activity-only without qualified stage movement. | Route to sales pipeline and strategic initiatives. | Reject diversification credit until buyer proof exists. |
| Capital overconcentration | Concentrated exposure asks for more cash, release, or capacity support without mitigation. | Route to capital allocation and founder decisions. | Reallocate or pause until exposure band improves. |
