## Validation Gate Rules

| Gate | Condition | Evidence required | Dashboard action | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prove | Assumption is materially influencing forecast, capital, release, or customer promise. | Current source record, owner, validation date, and fallback path. | Keep in active validation register. | Require dated proof before widening commitment. |
| Downgrade | Assumption misses validation date, source proof weakens, or owner evidence becomes stale. | Updated forecast, risk, variance, and source-board language. | Reduce confidence band and flag dependency heat. | Narrow commitment or pause related release. |
| Convert to variance | Assumption failure creates repeated drift or founder-rescued execution. | Plan variance record and owner accountability evidence. | Move from assumption watch to recovery review. | Set recovery owner and deadline. |
| Escalate to scenario | Failure would affect cash, supply, trust-linked release, or key counterparty timing. | Scenario response path and first-response owner. | Route to contingency board. | Approve fallback posture or containment. |
| Graduate | Assumption holds through validation, source evidence is current, and fallback is no longer primary. | KPI movement, source proof, and no unresolved stop rule. | Remove from fragile watch and retain as operating standard. | Permit broader operating reliance. |
