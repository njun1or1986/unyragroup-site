## Forecast Band Rules

| Forecast band | Required proof | Not allowed | Founder use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Committed | Signed or equivalent commercial commitment, release posture, cash path, margin view, and owner handoff are current. | Treating verbal confidence as committed revenue. | Use for operating plan and near-term cash expectations. |
| High-confidence | Pricing, payment, source, and customer timing evidence are current enough to support likely conversion. | Ignoring unresolved concessions, cash exposure, or source gaps. | Use for active founder attention and controlled capital planning. |
| Weighted | Deal is real but still depends on assumptions, source proof, or buyer action. | Consuming release capacity or cash support as if committed. | Use for monitored upside with proof gates. |
| Stretch | Early opportunity has strategic relevance but limited proof. | Allowing stretch to inflate plan quality or capital release. | Use for pipeline ambition only. |
| Downgraded | Proof weakens, source date slips, or confidence depends on founder rescue. | Leaving forecast band unchanged after evidence changes. | Route to recovery, risk, or owner accountability. |

## Revenue Quality Gates

| Gate | Required evidence | If current | If weak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pricing and margin gate | Pricing review, concession logic, profitability view, and recovery path are current. | Forecast band can hold. | Downgrade band or hold quote release. |
| Cash and collection gate | Cash forecast, collections path, credit posture, and release dependency are current. | Revenue can support cash planning. | Keep revenue out of committed cash assumptions. |
| Source and capacity gate | Supplier slot, backup posture, and shipment feasibility are current. | Weighted revenue can move toward high-confidence. | Keep revenue weighted or downgrade stretch. |
| Customer trust gate | Account posture, response quality, trust tier, and decision boundaries are current. | Customer commitment can support confidence. | Require founder decision or trust-posture review before widening. |
| Capital permission gate | Capital allocation confirms release, cash, and capacity support. | Forecast can influence resource priority. | Do not allocate scarce cash or release capacity to the deal. |
