## Cash Permission Bands

| Permission band | Condition | Founder posture | Required control action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cash-permitted | Collection proof, release posture, and outgoing funding sequence are current and aligned. | Movement can proceed inside approved limits. | Record owner, route, and next review date. |
| Cash-contained | Movement is strategically valid but depends on near-term collection or release proof. | Keep movement narrow and reversible. | Require collections, credit, funding, and capital support before widening. |
| Cash-watch | Forecast is acceptable, but timing, concentration, or margin quality remains sensitive. | Allow planning but not expansion. | Refresh source records before commitment language strengthens. |
| Cash-constrained | Stress gap or release-dependent exposure exceeds tolerance. | Prioritize liquidity over growth pressure. | Route to capital allocation, risk register, and founder decisions. |
| Cash-blocked | Movement would widen exposure without collection proof, release control, or downside response. | Stop movement until proof changes. | Record blocked action and recovery trigger. |

## Collections Proof Standard

| Proof type | Minimum evidence | Weak substitute to reject | Founder read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer promise | Named contact, amount, date, follow-up owner, and next escalation step. | General verbal comfort. | A promise is forecastable only when it has owner and date discipline. |
| Receivable quality | Aging status, dispute posture, credit posture, and release dependency. | A clean invoice without payment behavior context. | Cash quality depends on friction, not invoice value alone. |
| Release dependency | Release gate, blocked action, deposit logic, and next permissible move. | Operations urgency or relationship rationale. | Release should follow cash proof, not create cash dependency. |
| Cash concentration | Top-account share, linked exposure, and mitigation action. | Aggregate cash total without customer concentration read. | Liquidity is weaker when it depends on one account. |
| Margin quality | Profitability review, concession path, and realized economics. | Gross inflow without margin or recovery check. | Cash is healthier when margin quality is visible. |

| Rule | Condition | Required escalation | Founder action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collection slip stop | A top-account promise slips or loses owner/date clarity. | Route to collections, customer account, cash forecast, and risk register. | Downgrade cash confidence and restrict release expansion. |
| Release exposure stop | Release-dependent cash remains above threshold or widens. | Route to credit release, funding release, and capital allocation. | Hold non-priority movement until exposure falls. |

| Outgoing funding conflict | Shipment or payables request outruns collection proof. | Route to funding release and capital allocation. | Approve only top-priority funding or hold. |
