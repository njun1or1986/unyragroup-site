# 07 Archive

This layer is the retained-history command center for the UNYRA operating system. It preserves closed commercial, operating, compliance, governance, and proof records only after the active layer has released control with evidence, retrieval ownership, and a clear founder-readable closeout story.

## Archive Control Posture

| Control Field | Current Standard | Founder Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layer status | Retained history and closeout control layer | Use this hub to confirm what is closed, what is retrievable, and what must not be reopened casually. |
| Primary purpose | Preserve final matter history after active execution has ended. | Keeps prior decisions reconstructable without polluting live CRM, operations, compliance, or dashboard rooms. |
| Release rule | Nothing leaves the active layer until closeout evidence, residual risk, and retrieval ownership are clear. | Blocks archive from becoming a parking lot for unresolved work. |
| Retrieval rule | Every archived matter should tell a future reviewer what happened, where final records live, and who owns retrieval. | Makes archive useful for founder memory, audits, relationship review, and operating lessons. |
| Protection rule | Archive cleanup must not edit protected template masters or approved brand assets. | Preserves master-template and brand-control integrity while retained history matures. |

## Archive Readiness Gate

| Gate | Pass Standard | Founder Decision Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active ownership cleared | The live owner confirms no open task can still change commercial, operational, financial, compliance, claim, release, or retrieval outcome. | Determines whether the record can leave active operating control. |
| Evidence chain complete | Final deal, shipment, document-control, release, funding, compliance, decision, and dashboard routes agree when applicable. | Prevents archive finality from relying on memory or folder placement alone. |
| Residual risk named | Any watch item, precedent boundary, retrieval condition, or future-reference constraint is explicit. | Keeps future founder review from overstating what the closed matter proves. |
| Retrieval owner assigned | A named functional owner can recover final records without searching blindly. | Makes retained history operationally usable. |
| Closeout standard confirmed | The matter meets `05_sops/07_archive/Archive-Closeout-SOP.md` before archive release. | Keeps archive release governed by procedure instead of convenience. |

## Retrieval and Reopen Rules

| Retrieval Need | Use Archive When | Return to Active Layer When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prior commercial context | The founder needs final history, prior pricing logic, relationship memory, or bounded operating lessons. | A new commercial opportunity, negotiation, or customer action is being created. |
| Shipment or release proof | The founder needs to reconstruct what happened after closeout. | Movement, filing, payment, or release facts can still change. |
| Compliance or CAPA learning | The founder needs retained prevention, traceability, or regulatory history. | Inspection, claim, CAPA, supplier approval, or regulatory posture is still open. |
| Exception precedent | The founder needs to understand why an exception was contained. | A current matter wants to reuse the exception logic without fresh approval. |
| Audit or financing support | A retained record must support external diligence or audit memory. | Missing evidence requires owner follow-through or source-record repair. |

## Founder Review Cadence

| Cadence | Review Move | Closeout Standard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On archive request | Open the live matter, archive SOP, and proposed archive destination before release. | The founder can see why active control is no longer required. |
| Weekly while proof-watch remains | Reconcile retained archive language with linked live task, release, funding, shipment, or executable-check records. | Archive wording does not overstate finality. |
| Monthly | Confirm retained matters remain searchable, route-backed, and not carrying unresolved active work. | Archive stays useful as a retrieval layer. |
| Quarterly | Review whether any retained matter has become precedent for a current operating standard. | Lessons become SOP or dashboard updates only when source evidence supports them. |

## Protection and Escalation Rules

| Trigger | Required Action | Archive Protection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A source record contradicts archive finality | Keep the record in proof-watch posture until sources agree. | Protects trust in retained history. |
| A live task, claim, payment, release, or compliance item remains open | Return the matter to the active layer before archive release. | Prevents unresolved work from disappearing. |
| A future matter cites archived exception logic as precedent | Require founder decision, risk, integrity, and executable-check comparison before reuse. | Prevents past exceptions from becoming default operating behavior. |
| Retrieval requires blind searching across folders | Add route-backed retrieval ownership before archive finality. | Keeps archive founder-readable and audit-ready. |
| Archive cleanup touches protected templates or approved brand assets | Stop and preserve the protected source; create or update only live working records. | Maintains template and brand protections. |
