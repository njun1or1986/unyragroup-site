# Brand

This layer governs UNYRA's approved brand system across assets, written rules, export standards, preview benchmarks, and retained brand history. It is the founder-facing entry point when visual identity, presentation governance, or outbound benchmark quality needs to be reviewed before work moves into live operating surfaces.

## Open first

| Priority | Surface | Founder use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `OPEN-HERE.html` | Open the governed brand portal before reviewing assets, guidelines, exports, or retained brand history. |

## Brand structure

| Brand surface | Governance role |
| --- | --- |
| `01_assets/OPEN-HERE.html` | Approved logos, signatures, compositions, and retained brand assets. |
| `02_guidelines/OPEN-HERE.html` | Identity rules, document standard, and institutional voice discipline. |
| `03_exports/OPEN-HERE.html` | Preview docs, domain lanes, visual standard, and founder-facing benchmark output. |
| `99_archive/` | Retired brand material that should remain outside the active system. |

## Founder routes

| Founder need | Route decision |
| --- | --- |
| Approved marks, signatures, or compositions | Enter through `01_assets/OPEN-HERE.html`. |
| Written identity, document-design, or voice rules | Enter through `02_guidelines/OPEN-HERE.html`. |
| Preview benchmarks, domain lanes, or visual-governance routing | Enter through `03_exports/OPEN-HERE.html`. |

## Governance rule

| Layer | Governance rule |
| --- | --- |
| Assets | Define what may be used. |
| Guidelines | Define how the brand should sound and behave. |
| Exports | Define how the brand appears in founder-facing benchmark surfaces. |
| Archive | Preserves retired material without letting it pollute active decisions. |

## Protection note

| Protection | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Identity map | Brand is the governed identity map, not a loose design folder. |
| Surface ownership | Every mark, rule, export, and retired asset should remain in its owned surface. |
| Founder traceability | Founder review should trace presentation quality back to the approved source and current visual standard. |
