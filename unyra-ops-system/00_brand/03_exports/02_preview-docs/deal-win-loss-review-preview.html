Outcome Snapshot

What happened commercially and how the customer decided

Quoted Structure

1,200 export cases / FOB Qingdao / 30% deposit and balance against shipping documents / expected margin 16.8%.

Award Result

Customer awarded the first shipment to an incumbent supplier after requesting longer payment flexibility and a lower landed equivalent.

Primary Competitor

Regional importer already carrying the lane with a faster booking commitment and open-account comfort for the buyer.

Re-Entry Posture

Medium-high. Buyer asked UNYRA to stay close for the next rotation if market softens or incumbent quality slips again.

Review Control Matrix

Linked records, missed signals, and the break point

Linked Source Records

Lead intake complete / customer onboarding still active / internal deal summary issued / proforma sent but no contract release.

Primary Break Point

Customer wanted more flexible payment positioning before committing to a first container with a new supplier relationship.

Signal Missed

Buyer repeatedly emphasized first-shipment cash protection; we treated it as negotiable instead of as the main award filter.

Re-Entry Trigger

Reopen immediately if incumbent misses quality consistency or if buyer requests a second-source contingency quote for July intake.

Evidence and Learning

What the result teaches the commercial team

Evidence Point Observation Commercial Lesson Owner
Pricing feedback Buyer indicated the incumbent landed about USD 18 per case lower on the initial award round. Pre-quote market anchoring must be tighter before promising a viable opening margin. Commercial Desk
Payment posture Customer favored lower upfront cash exposure for the first shipment. New-account offers need a defined credit strategy or staged structure before negotiation advances. Founder and Finance
Speed to confidence Competitor offered a faster execution story based on prior lane history. For first-rotation deals, we need a stronger proof pack on execution reliability, not only pricing logic. Sales and Operations

Commercial Diagnosis

Why the deal was lost

The loss was not caused by one variable alone. Pricing was directionally high, but the deeper issue was first-shipment confidence: the buyer wanted both commercial relief and proof that switching suppliers would not create operational risk.

Founder Takeaway

What should change next time

On new accounts, UNYRA should package price, payment structure, and execution proof as one offer. If any one of those stays weak, the incumbent advantage remains too strong for a first award.
