Claims and Remedies

How exceptions are handled

Claims must be raised within the agreed review period with photo, lot, and shipment evidence. Commercial remedy is limited to verified non-conformance directly tied to the contracted specification and document set.

Legal Risk Controls

Key protections inside the agreement

Force majeure, amendment control, assignment restrictions, and dispute-resolution language ensure that commercial changes are handled through controlled written updates instead of informal operational messages.
