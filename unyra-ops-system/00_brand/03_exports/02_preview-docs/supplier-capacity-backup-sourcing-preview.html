How To Use This

What this review is for

This layer is not just another supplier scorecard. It is the founder's view of whether approved supply can actually hold under scale, disruption, or sudden program pressure.

Use it to decide when to diversify suppliers, when to formalize fallback sources, and when current execution quality plus only controlled trust posture is creating false confidence about resilience.
