Ownership Map

Who controls the handoff after the win

Commercial owns the final promise set, purchasing owns supplier release, logistics owns shipment opening, compliance owns filing readiness, and finance owns deposit confirmation. This handoff exists so those responsibilities start aligned instead of being reconstructed later.

Launch Risk

Main risk in the first 48 hours

The key risk is releasing purchasing before the execution packet is synchronized. If the PO opens before payment tagging, document control, and filing assumptions are aligned, version confusion and deadline compression follow immediately.
