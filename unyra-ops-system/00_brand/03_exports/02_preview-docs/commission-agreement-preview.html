Triggers and Exclusions

What creates a right to commission and what does not

Area Agreed Position Control Note
Trigger Event Commission arises only on qualifying sales to covered accounts introduced or maintained under the agreement. Deal linkage must be documented in the CRM and legal record set.
Revenue Basis Calculated on net collected revenue after approved credits, returns, and non-recoverable discounts. Uncollected invoices and disputed balances are excluded until resolved and paid.
Exclusions No commission on house accounts, direct pre-existing relationships, or business outside covered scope. Scope disputes are resolved against the written covered-account schedule.
Non-circumvention Applicable only to listed covered accounts during the term and any defined tail period. Does not create blanket exclusivity beyond the written scope.

Reporting and Evidence

How payment support is documented

Commission statements should be issued on a monthly cycle with supporting invoice references, collection confirmation, approved credit adjustments, and a clear calculation basis for each covered account.
