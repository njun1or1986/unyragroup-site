Purpose and Covered Information

Why the agreement exists and what it protects

Primary Business Purpose

Evaluation of a repeat-volume sourcing and distribution program covering garlic, ginger, pricing strategy, and operating structure.

Covered Information

Pricing, customer and supplier identities, specifications, shipment planning, compliance methods, samples, and internal commercial analysis.

Standard Exclusions

Information already public, independently developed without use of protected data, or lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

Permitted Representatives

Employees, legal advisors, finance staff, and consultants with a need to know and confidentiality obligations no weaker than this agreement.

Use and Protection Obligations

How protected information must be handled

Control Area Requirement Operational Note
Non-disclosure No disclosure outside permitted representatives without prior written consent. Operational summaries and pricing files must stay inside controlled deal records.
Limited Use Use only for the defined evaluation and negotiation purpose. Protected information cannot be reused for unrelated sourcing or sales campaigns.
Access Control Reasonable safeguards must protect digital and printed information. Only approved working copies should circulate inside the operating system.
Compelled Disclosure Prompt notice required if disclosure is legally compelled, where permitted by law. The disclosing party must have a chance to seek protective relief.

Relationship Controls

Commercial boundaries around shared information

Non-circumvention and information-retention rules may be added for sensitive supplier and buyer introductions. Return or certified destruction applies at the end of the relationship except where limited retention is required by law or audit.

Legal Risk Controls

Core protective clauses

Ownership of information stays with the disclosing party, no license is implied, equitable relief remains available, and amendments only take effect through controlled written execution by both parties.
