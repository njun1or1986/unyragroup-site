Open Gaps and Revision Risks

What could break packet integrity

No live packet gaps remain in this sample issue. The main control risk is accidental circulation of older commercial invoice or packing list versions outside the register. If any shipment-facing field changes, the full packet should be reissued under a new logged revision rather than patched informally.

Escalate immediately if a broker, customer, or supplier is working from a prior revision, or if a filing proceeds against an unregistered packet set, or if executable gate evidence cannot be tied back to the controlled issue.
