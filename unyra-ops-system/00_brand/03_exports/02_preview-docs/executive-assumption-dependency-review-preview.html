How To Use This

What this layer is for

This layer makes the hidden logic behind forecast, plan, and contingency explicit. It tells the founder what is currently being assumed, when it must be validated, and what happens if it proves false.

Use it to stop fragile assumptions from being mistaken for reliable operating truth, especially when cash timing, supplier readiness, or same-day live-gate behavior begin to drift.
