How To Use This

What this layer is for

This layer gives the founder one disciplined view of all temporary deviations that are still live, especially when one matter is accumulating pricing, release, claims, and control exceptions at the same time.

Use it to force expiry logic, blocked-action discipline, recovery paths, and return-to-standard proof before exceptions normalize into habit.
