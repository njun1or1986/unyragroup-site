Variance Diagnosis

Why margin moved below plan

The margin did not collapse because operations failed. Most of the variance came from a deliberate commercial concession to secure a first-order relationship, followed by a smaller-than-expected supplier and logistics overrun.

Founder Takeaway

What should change next cycle

UNYRA should explicitly price first-order relationship concessions as a governed margin decision. If the founder wants to invest in the account, that should appear as a planned strategy, not as hidden variance afterward.
