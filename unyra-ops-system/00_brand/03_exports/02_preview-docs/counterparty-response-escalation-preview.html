Escalation Ladder

Institutional follow-up sequence

Stage 1

Controlled initial release with explicit deadline and return path.

Stage 2

Formal reminder against the same communication reference before SLA expiry drifts too far.

Stage 3

Escalation notice plus secondary channel, with consequence clearly stated.

Stage 4

Hold, revalidate, or elevate to founder or executive control before the matter keeps moving.
