Archive Control Matrix

Retention and retrieval discipline

Linked customer, supplier, deal, shipment, complaint, and traceability references are preserved in one closeout file. Executable gate evidence is also preserved so future commercial, audit, or dispute review can reconstruct not only what moved, but how it was finally cleared to move. Retrieval ownership is assigned to the founder office and compliance desk so future commercial, audit, or dispute review can happen without reconstructing the matter from scratch.
