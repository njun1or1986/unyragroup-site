How To Use This

What this layer is for

This layer turns the top executive risks into actual trigger-response logic, not just visibility. It tells the founder what should happen first when cash, sourcing, pricing, execution, or live-gate pressure intensifies.

Use it to pre-decide hard trade-offs, compress response time, and reduce the chance that real pressure gets handled through improvised exceptions or founder rescue instead of controlled contingency logic.
