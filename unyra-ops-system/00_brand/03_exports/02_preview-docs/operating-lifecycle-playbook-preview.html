Lifecycle Spine

The intended order of record creation

Stage Trigger Required Record Exit Rule
Lead Real commercial angle exists Lead Intake Clear opportunity and next discovery step
Customer or Supplier Onboarding Relationship is credible enough for structured evaluation Customer Intake or Supplier Intake Operational and compliance fit understood
Deal Activation Product, quantity, or negotiation path becomes real Internal Deal Summary Commercial assumptions and ownership are clear
Offer and Contract Formal pricing or commitment cycle starts Proforma and Sales Contract Written confirmation and controlled issue exist
Execution Purchasing or shipment coordination begins PO, shipment folder, logistics packet Operational and compliance controls are live
Compliance and Incident Control Filing or quality control becomes active ISF, FSVP, Prior Notice, complaint, traceability Evidence and resolution are auditable
Archive Matter is fully complete and inactive Archive move No live blocker, filing, or dispute remains open

Task Rule

When work becomes a task record

If the work has a real owner, deadline, and blocker or decision point, it should become a governed task. That prevents revenue, timing, and compliance actions from getting lost in chat, notes, or inboxes.

Dashboard Rule

What always updates last

Dashboards and command centers update only after the underlying CRM, operations, compliance, and task records are current. This preserves source-of-truth discipline and keeps founder views trustworthy.

Control Principles

Rules that keep the repository coherent

Sequence Rule

Do not skip stages without a controlled reason

A deal should not appear before the relationship is real, and execution should not appear before commitment is controlled.

Stage discipline

Linkage Rule

Every meaningful record should point to its linked record set

Lead to customer, customer to deal, deal to shipment, shipment to compliance, complaint back to all relevant entities.

Traceability

Archive Rule

Archive only after full closure, never as a cleanup shortcut

Open shipments, live approvals, and unresolved incidents stay active until the business risk is genuinely finished.

Closure control
