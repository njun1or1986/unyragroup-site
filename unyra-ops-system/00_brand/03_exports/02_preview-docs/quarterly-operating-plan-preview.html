Risk and Trade-Off Review

What could bend the quarter off course

Constraint or Risk Founder Trade-Off
Cash timing still depends on a small number of accounts Slow some release logic until cash quality improves.
Supplier resilience lags current execution quality Invest time in backup qualification before pushing volume harder.
Revenue concentration remains too high Prioritize diversification even if it slows short-term volume.
Strategic counterparties still require too much reminder and escalation pressure Escalate faster, hold some releases earlier, and reprice trust assumptions more explicitly.
Same-day gate behavior still depends on founder rescue on some trust-linked matters Slow widening and force executable-check usage until owner behavior becomes normal.
Pricing exceptions can weaken economics quickly Say no more often when recovery logic is weak.

How To Use This

What this plan is for

This layer is not a replacement for monthly reviews or initiative tracking. It is the founder's quarterly contract with the business about what quality of growth, cash, supply, counterparty behavior, and live-gate discipline should look like over the next 90 days.

Use it to turn strategy into quarter commitments, named owners, and decision thresholds before the next quarter begins, especially when the business needs less founder rescue at its most sensitive gates.
