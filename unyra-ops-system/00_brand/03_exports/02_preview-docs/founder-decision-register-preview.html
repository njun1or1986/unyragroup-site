Decision Snapshot

What the founder is actually being asked to decide

Triggering Issue

The deal needs both a strategic pricing concession and a controlled release posture tied to deposit timing while customer trust quality is still fragile.

Decision Required

Approve the guarded concession logic and allow proforma release only under the linked payment-control and trust-override conditions.

Impact

Affects first-order account entry, gross-margin posture, PO release timing, and downstream shipment launch readiness.

Trust Override Class

This is a founder-approved growth override under fragile trust, not a normalization of the account into standard release comfort.

Override Rationale

The founder is choosing to proceed because the strategic upside is real, but only as a bounded exception while trust posture remains below normal release comfort.

What Stays Blocked

No open release acceleration, no unsecured payment softening, and no blanket scale-up beyond the guarded first-order path.

Current Recommendation

Approve as a founder-tagged investment decision, not as a normal margin setting or normal-trust release, and enforce follow-through review on the next cycle.

Strategic Matter View

Pricing governance review active / credit and release control active / trust posture matrix active / concession and release risks documented.

Trust and Economic Use

Decision must feed the trust posture matrix and later trade profitability review so founder-approved overrides do not disappear into unexplained margin variance or silent trust drift.

Override Control Discipline

The founder decision, trust matrix, credit control, and exception review must all show the same override class, blocked actions, recheck date, and return-to-standard trigger.

Decision Logic

Why this belongs at founder level

This is not a routine commercial choice. It changes the economic nature of the trade and sets the tone for how aggressively UNYRA is willing to invest in opening a strategic account while trust quality is still below normal comfort. That trade-off belongs in a recorded founder decision, not in scattered notes.

Decision Rule

What should happen after approval

Once approved, the decision should immediately trigger the linked proforma release, preserve the payment gate, preserve the trust-override discipline, keep broader release expansion blocked, and open a required profitability follow-up so the account-investment logic is reviewed against reality later.
