Main Lesson

What should be repeated

The strongest result came from synchronized control across launch handoff, packet governance, and pre-shipment release. Because those upstream gates were disciplined and their executable evidence was retained, the arrival stage stayed quiet and predictable and the matter can now be reconstructed cleanly later.

Operational Watchout

What still deserves attention next cycle

The team should still shorten the time between customer receipt confirmation and formal shipment review closure. Waiting too long weakens memory quality and delays usable feedback into supplier and customer governance.
