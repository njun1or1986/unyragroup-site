How To Use This

What this layer is for

This layer tells the founder where scarce cash, supplier bandwidth, and release approvals should go first. It exists to force real choice between committed revenue credibility and speculative upside.

Use it to decide what moves now, what stays conditional, and what should be deferred until cash quality, payment structure, supply coverage, and trust posture become strong enough to support a cleaner release.
