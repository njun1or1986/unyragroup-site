Main Operational Risk

What could still break release quality

Even when the shipment is technically ready, the real risk is drift between the controlled packet and any ad hoc version shared outside the register. A release review prevents the team from treating “mostly ready” as operationally safe when trust-linked release consequences are still active in the background.

Control Rule

How UNYRA should use this gate

Use this review at the moment a shipment is about to move from internal preparation into broker, forwarder, or customer-facing release. It is the final quality screen between internal readiness and external execution, including material customer or supplier trust posture that can still change the release decision.
